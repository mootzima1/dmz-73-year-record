**받는 곳**: `mopix@nara.gov` (National Archives at College Park – Motion Pictures)
**참고 전화**: +1 (301) 837-3540

> **보내기 전 확인**: 상업적 이용임을 숨기지 말 것. 숨기면 돌아온 답변이 우리 용도에 무효가 된다.

```
Subject: Rights status inquiry — four Korean Armistice / Panmunjom moving image items (NAIDs 102045422, 102047435, 86454, 140105180)

Dear Motion Picture Reference Staff,

I am a documentary producer in the Republic of Korea. I am researching footage
for a short historical documentary about the Korean Demilitarized Zone, covering
the 1953 Armistice through the present day. The film will be published on a
monetized YouTube channel, so this is a commercial use.

In the National Archives Catalog, the following four items show "Access:
Unrestricted" but their use status is either "Possibly Restricted (Copyright)"
or "Undetermined." I would be grateful for clarification before I proceed.

  1. NAID 102045422 — "Military Armistice Commission, Panmunjom, Korea"
     Local ID 111-DD-141-63 | RG 111, Filmed News Releases
     Catalog shows: Use — Possibly Restricted (Copyright)

  2. NAID 102047435 — "U.S. Official Killed in Panmunjom, Korea"
     Local ID 111-DD-67-76 | RG 111, Filmed News Releases
     Catalog shows: Use — Possibly Restricted (Copyright)

  3. NAID 86454 — "PANMUNJOM STORY"
     Local ID 428-NPC-40219 | RG 428, Moving Images Relating to Military Activities
     Catalog shows: Use — Undetermined

  4. NAID 140105180 — "To Help Peace Survive"
     Local ID 330-DIMOC-FTBEL1226 | RG 330, Moving Images Related to
     Combat Visual Information (produced 1970)
     Catalog shows: Use — Possibly Restricted (Copyright)

My specific questions:

  a) For each item, is NARA aware of any non-federal or third-party material
     (for example, commercial newsreel footage licensed into the compilation)
     that would carry rights separate from the U.S. Government?

  b) If an item is believed to be entirely a U.S. Government work, may I treat
     it as being in the public domain for commercial documentary use, and is
     there a credit line NARA prefers?

  c) For item 4, which was produced in 1970 but appears to incorporate earlier
     footage of the 1953 Armistice signing: is the provenance of the embedded
     historical footage documented anywhere in your holdings?

  d) Regarding item 3 specifically: the catalog records it as unedited
     ("have not been edited") and silent, produced by the Naval Photographic
     Center. As original unedited camera footage rather than a compiled news
     release, it would appear unlikely to contain licensed third-party
     material. Would you agree that this item can be treated as a U.S.
     Government work, and could its use status be updated from "Undetermined"?

  e) Are higher-resolution preservation or reference copies available for
     purchase or download for any of these items, and what is the procedure?

Thank you very much for your time. I am happy to provide any further detail
about the project.

Kind regards,
[이름]
[직함 / 채널명]
[이메일]
[전화]
```
